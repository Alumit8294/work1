Our Story

Designing modern simplicity and timeless craftsmanship for every living space.

Who We Are

Crafting Comfort with Intentional Design

Founded in 2016, Lumina began with a single vision: to bridge the gap between minimalist aesthetics and everyday functionality. We believe that furniture shouldn't just occupy space—it should enhance the way you live.

Every piece in our collection is thoughtfully sculpted by skilled artisans using responsibly sourced natural woods, premium textiles, and eco-friendly finishes. We design for longevity, creating modern heirlooms that move with you through life.

What Drives Us

Our core principles guide every piece we bring into your home.

Artisanal Quality

Built by master craftspeople who take pride in precision joinery, rich textures, and flawless structural integrity.

Sustainable Sourcing

We work exclusively with FSC-certified wood mills and eco-conscious fabric suppliers to minimize our footprint.

Human-Centered

Designed around real daily living—prioritizing ergonomic comfort, intuitive assembly, and effortless upkeep.

Sustainable Workshop

Thoughtfully Made from Forest to Living Room

We reject throwaway culture. By combining modern engineering with traditional woodworking techniques, Lumina furniture is engineered to endure decades of family dinners, cozy movie nights, and quiet mornings.

We plant five trees for every single piece of wooden furniture crafted, helping restore vulnerable forestry systems around the globe.
